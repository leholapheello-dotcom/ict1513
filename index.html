About Us

Ha-Ntate Tladi is a small, community-owned vegetable stall operating at the corner of Mampoi Road & Nkahloleng Road, Phuthaditjhaba-A, right between two of the area’s busiest shopping complexes, Maluti Crescent Mall and Setsing Plaza. Every morning the stall is restocked with fresh, hand-picked potatoes, onions, tomatoes, cabbages, chillies, and oranges, sourced from trusted local suppliers so that customers always find something crisp and in season. The stall has become a familiar stop for shoppers walking between the two malls, many of whom now plan their grocery run specifically around a visit to Ha-Ntate Tladi.

What began as a small table of sweets and snacks has grown, over several years, into a trusted vegetable business built on consistency and fair pricing. The owner noticed early on that selling snacks to schoolchildren meant income dropped sharply during school holidays, so the business shifted to fresh produce — a category of goods that people need every single day of the year, holiday or not. That single decision steadied the business and allowed it to grow a loyal, everyday customer base rather than one tied to the school calendar. Right now, every customer who buys three bags of vegetables gets a fourth bag absolutely free — ask at the stall for today’s special.

Beyond selling vegetables, Ha-Ntate Tladi also runs a convenient parcel counter for just R5 per person, so shoppers can leave their bags safely at the stall while they walk freely between both malls without carrying heavy groceries around all day. The stall is positioned close to public transport routes, which makes it easy for customers to get home afterwards with everything they have bought. This mix of fresh produce and practical services has made the stall much more than just a place to buy vegetables — it has become a small, dependable fixture of daily life in Phuthaditjhaba-A.

The business also gives back to the community it serves. Vegetables that are slightly bruised or otherwise unfit for sale are never simply thrown away; they are given to homeless individuals in the area, along with packaging material so that they, too, can resell what they receive if they choose to. This small act of generosity reflects the values behind Ha-Ntate Tladi — a business built not only to make a living, but to support the wider community around it, one bag of vegetables at a time.
