His Story

After being retrenched from his position as a security guard at a tertiary institution, Ha-Ntate Tladi spent years applying for employment without success. Rather than give up, he chose entrepreneurship.

He started small, selling sweets and snacks. After careful analysis he noticed revenue dropped during school holidays because his primary customers were schoolchildren. He pivoted to selling vegetables — reasoning that food is a universal necessity unaffected by school calendars.

Positioned near two busy shopping complexes, he built a consistent, loyal customer base. He also donates cracked or unsellable tomatoes to homeless individuals in the area, or gives them packaging material so they can sell the tomatoes themselves.

Business Philosophy

  • Always stock fresh, quality produce
  • Keep prices affordable and fair for the community
  • Give back at every opportunity
  • Build trust through consistency and honesty
  • Serve every customer with dignity and respect
